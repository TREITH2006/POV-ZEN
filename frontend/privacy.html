Privacy Policy

This page describes, in plain terms, what information POV-ZEN collects and how it is used. It is a summary for residents and PG owners, not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

Account details (name, email, phone), PG membership details (room number, sharing type), content you submit (issue reports and photos, food feedback, rent records, documents) and basic usage timestamps.

How it is used

Solely to operate the features of your own PG group: showing menus and announcements, routing issue reports to your PG owner, tracking rent status, and sharing documents your owner has uploaded.

Who can see it

Information is strictly isolated to your own PG group. Residents of a different PG can never see your PG's data, and vice versa — this is enforced by the application itself, not just hidden in the interface. Within your own group, an Owner can see information relevant to managing the PG (residents, issues, rent, documents); other residents cannot see your private issue reports.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with any other PG group or outside party.

Questions or concerns

Use "Help / Report Website Issue" in the footer of any page, or contact your PG owner directly for anything specific to your PG.
